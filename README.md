# Sales Analysis using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview 

This project contains sales analysis reports created using Microsoft Excel as part of my data analytics learning journey.

The analysis focuses on customer performance, market performance against targets, and top-performing products.

## 📊 Reports

### 1. Customer Performance

Analyzes customer-level sales performance to identify:

- Customer Sales contribution over the years (2019 - 2021) 
- Comparing Net Sales performance between the years 2020 and 2021
- Sales growth percentage

### 2. Market Performance vs Target

Compares actual sales performance against the assigned market targets
to identify:

- Whether markets are meeting their targets
- Markets that are falling below their targets
- Sales variance against target

### 3. Top 10 Products

Identifies the top 10 products based on the percentage increase in their net sales from 2020 to 2021

- Best-selling products 
- Product-level performance
- Comparing Product sales percentages between years 2020 and 2021

### 4. Division Level Report

To present the net sales data for 2020 and 2021 at the division level

- N&S - Network and Storage
- P&A - Peripheral and Accessories
- PC - Personal Computer
- Comparison of Sales data along with growth percentage

### 5. Top and Bottom 5 Products

Lists the most and least sold products

- Products ranked in the top and bottom 5 in terms of quantity sold
- Highlights the quantity sold from highest to lowest for the top 5 and lowest to highest for the bottom 5

### 6. New products 2021

Classifies the new products that Atliq began selling in 2021

- New products from 2021
- Net sales value for each product

### 7. Top 5 Countries 2021

Identifies the top 5 countries by net sales in 2021

- Highlights their net sales values, ranked from highest to lowest
