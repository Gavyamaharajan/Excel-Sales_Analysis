# Sales Analysis using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview 

This project contains sales analysis reports created using Microsoft Excel as part of my data analytics learning journey.
The analysis focuses on customer performance, market performance against targets, and top-performing products.

## 📊 Reports

### 1. Customer Performance

_[Customer Performance Report](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)_
- Customer Sales contribution over the years (2019 - 2021) 
- Comparing Net Sales performance between the years 2020 and 2021

### 2. Market Performance vs Target

_[Market Performance vs Target](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target.pdf)_
- Markets that are falling below their targets
- Sales variance against target

### 3. Top 10 Products

_[Top 10 Products](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Top10_products.pdf)_
- The top 10 products based on the percentage increase in their net sales from 2020 to 2021

### 4. Division Level Report

_[Division Level Report](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Division_level_report.pdf)_

_[New Products of 2021](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/New_products_2021.pdf)_

_[Top 5 Countries in 2021](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Top_5countries_2021.pdf)_

## 🎯 Objective
The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Excel.

- **Purpose of sales analytics:** Empower businesses to monitor and evaluate their sales activities and performance.

- **Importance of analyzing sales data:** Identify sales patterns and track key performance indicators (KPIs).

- **Role of reports:** Determine effective customer discounts, facilitate negotiations with consumers, and identify potential business expansion opportunities in promising countries.
