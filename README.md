# Sales Analysis Reports using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview 

This project contains sales analysis reports created using Microsoft Excel as part of my data analytics learning journey.
The analysis focuses on customer performance, market performance against sales targets, product performance, and sales growth across different business dimensions.

## 📊 Reports

### 1. _[Customer Performance Report](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)_
- Customer Sales contribution over the years (2019 - 2021) 
- Comparison of Net Sales performance between the years 2020 and 2021

### 2. _[Market Performance vs Sales Target](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Market%20Performance%20vs%20Target.pdf)_
- Markets performing below their sales targets
- Sales variance against target

### 3. _[Top 10 Products](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Top10_products.pdf)_
- The top 10 products based on the percentage increase in their net sales from 2020 to 2021

### 4. _[Division Level Report](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Division_level_report.pdf)_
- To present the net sales data for 2020 and 2021 at the division level along with growth percentage
- N&S - Network and Storage
- P&A - Peripheral and Accessories
- PC - Personal Computer

### 5. _[Top and Bottom 5 Products](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Top_bottom_5products.pdf)_
- Products ranked in the top and bottom 5 based on quantity sold

### 6. _[New Products of 2021](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/New_products_2021.pdf)_
- Identifies products introduced by Atliq in 2021 and their Net sales value

### 7. _[Top 5 Countries in 2021](https://github.com/Gavyamaharajan/Excel-Sales_Analysis/blob/main/Top_5countries_2021.pdf)_
- Identifies the top 5 countries by net sales in 2021
- Highlights their net sales values, ranked from highest to lowest

## 🎯 Objective
This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Excel.

The analysis aims to:

- Monitor and evaluate sales performance
- Identify sales trends and patterns
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Compare actual sales performance against targets

Role of reports:

- Determine effective customer discounts
- Facilitate negotiations with consumers
- Identify potential business expansion opportunities in promising countries
